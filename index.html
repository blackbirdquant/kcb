Colorado's Adopt-a-Highway Programs

Keep Colorado's roadsides clean — together.

Keep Colorado Beautiful connects businesses and communities to the highways they care about — through CDOT's Clean Colorado sponsorships and Spruce Up Colorado volunteer adoptions.

Explore the programs ↓
Two ways to take part

Find your fit

Sponsored · professional cleaning

Clean Colorado

Businesses, organizations, and individuals fund litter removal on a one-mile highway segment — at no cost to taxpayers. Professional crews clean it 12–26× a year, and your name or logo goes on the recognition sign.

Volunteer · community cleaning

Spruce Up Colorado

Your group adopts a segment and keeps it litter-free. CDOT assigns the section, installs recognition signs, and provides trash bags, safety vests, and the required safety training.
